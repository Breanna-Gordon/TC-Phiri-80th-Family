A Life Well Lived
A legacy of love, wisdom, and devotion — 80 years of God's favor. Today we honor a life that has poured strength into generations, a heart that has nurtured family, and a spirit that continues to shine with grace.

May this celebration remind us of the beauty of a life lived faithfully, the joy of family gathered in unity, and the blessing of witnessing God's goodness through every season.

“For with You is the fountain of life; in Your light we see light.” — Psalm 36:9

With gratitude, we celebrate Mrs. T.C. Phiri — a devoted wife, mother, grandmother, sister, friend, and pillar of faith whose journey continues to inspire us all.

Getting Around Ndola

Ndola is served by Simon Mwansa Kapwepwe International Airport (NLA). Guests may arrange airport pick-ups through hotel shuttles or private taxi operators at the arrival area.

  • Hotel Shuttle — available at Protea & The Urban Hotel
  • Private Car Hire — Avis, Europcar Zambia, Owen's Car Hire
  • Local Taxis — bookable by phone or hotel concierge, ideal for hops between hotels, Njele Country Park, the Cathedral, and the Phiri Residence
  • Ride-Hailing Apps — Ulendo Taxi & Yango, for safe transport with upfront pricing
